Privacy Policy

How Mona handles information

Mona is an internal menu-management application used by Pizzeria Lidan AB to synchronize restaurant and menu information between Lidan's systems and the Uber Eats Marketplace API.

Effective and last updated: 24 August 2026

Current scope

Store and menu data only

Mona currently uses the Eats Marketplace store and menu capabilities. It is not a customer ordering, payment, delivery, advertising, or profiling application.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Mona and its connection to the Uber Eats Marketplace API. It explains what information Mona processes, why it is processed, how long it is kept, and the choices available to people whose personal data may be involved.

Mona is a private operational tool for authorized personnel. It is separate from the public Lidan Pizzeria ordering website and from Uber's own applications and services.

3. Information we process

Mona is designed to process the minimum information needed to manage the integration.

Restaurant and store information

Uber store identifiers, restaurant name, location, store configuration, supported fulfillment types, and operational settings made available through the authorized API connection.

Menu and catalog information

Menus, categories, items, names, descriptions, prices, VAT, availability, images, modifier groups, and modifier options. This is primarily business information rather than information about an individual.

Authentication and security information

OAuth access tokens, application and store connection status, authorized-user identifiers where supplied, and security events needed to protect the integration. Client secrets and access tokens are treated as confidential credentials.

Operational records

Request times, action types, entity identifiers, validation results, approval records, response status codes, errors, and limited technical logs used for reliability, auditing, and incident investigation.

What Mona does not currently request

Mona's current menu-sync function is not designed to request or store eater names, eater contact details, delivery addresses, courier information, payment-card details, or order histories. Mona does not currently request Eats Order API scopes. If Mona's scope expands to order or consumer information, this policy will be updated before that processing begins.

4. How and why we use information

We use information processed by Mona to:

  • connect the authorized Lidan restaurant location to Uber Eats;
  • read, compare, validate, and synchronize store and menu data;
  • prepare human-reviewable change plans before production updates;
  • prevent accidental or unauthorized changes;
  • verify completed updates and support recovery;
  • monitor reliability, investigate errors, and maintain security;
  • meet applicable legal, accounting, and contractual obligations.

Where the processing involves personal data, our legal bases may include our legitimate interests in operating and securing our restaurant systems, performance of an agreement, and compliance with legal obligations. We do not use information obtained through the Uber API for targeted advertising or unrelated profiling.

5. Where information comes from

Information processed by Mona may come from:

  • Pizzeria Lidan AB's own menu and restaurant systems;
  • the Uber Eats Marketplace API under approved scopes;
  • authorized personnel who configure or approve a synchronization;
  • technical systems that generate security and operational logs.

6. When information is shared

We may disclose limited information to:

  • Uber, where necessary to authenticate the integration and manage the connected Uber Eats store and menu;
  • hosting, infrastructure, security, and support providers acting on our instructions and subject to appropriate safeguards;
  • professional advisers, public authorities, or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and security.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share Uber API data with data brokers or use it for third-party advertising.

Uber separately determines how it processes information within its own services. See the Uber Privacy Notice.

7. How long information is kept

  • Access tokens are retained only while needed for the authorized connection and are removed or made unusable when the integration is disconnected or access is revoked.
  • Current menu configuration and necessary recovery snapshots are kept while the integration is active. Superseded recovery snapshots are normally deleted within 90 days.
  • Routine operational and security logs are normally retained for up to 90 days.
  • Records may be kept longer when reasonably required for a security investigation, dispute, audit, or legal obligation.

We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed for the purposes described above.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. These include encrypted network connections, restricted access, least-privilege API scopes, secret separation, validation before changes, audit records, and separation of test and production environments.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. If we identify a security incident involving personal data, we will investigate and notify affected parties and authorities when required by law.

9. International transfers

Uber and some technology providers may process information outside Sweden or the European Economic Area. Where required, we use legally recognized safeguards such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

10. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Some rights may be limited where we must retain information for security, legal, or contractual reasons.

Contact us using the details below to exercise a right. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
